About us

CP Norfolk

/ see-pee nor-fuk / noun, India. Also: CPNI

  1. A joint venture between CP Group, Thailand, and Norfolk Foods, Sri Lanka.
  2. A plant in Chittoor, Andhra Pradesh, making chicken and vegetarian frozen foods.
  3. Farm to fork: one integrated supply chain, with quality checks at every stage.

CP Group, Thailand

Started in Bangkok by two hardworking brothers. More than 100 years on, Charoen Pokphand Foods and its subsidiaries are a leading agro-industrial and food group, and one of the largest food companies in Asia.

Norfolk Foods, Sri Lanka

The brainchild of Mohamed Ziauddin, a British-trained food specialist. Norfolk has brought value-added meat, poultry, fish and vegetarian products to star-class hotels for over three decades.

We are committed to products that are nutritious, tasty, safe, and of the highest quality.

Asked before the first order.

Who is behind CP Norfolk India?

A joint venture between CP Group, Thailand, and Norfolk Foods, Sri Lanka.

Where are the products manufactured?

At our plant at Plot No. 19, 20, 21 & 22, APIIC Industrial Park, G D Nellore, Chittoor, Andhra Pradesh 517125.

What pack size do you supply?

Every product in the brochure range comes in a 1 kg food service pack. The catalogue above lists pieces per pack and weight per piece.

Are the products halal?

Yes. The range is halal certified. The plant is FSSC 22000 certified and FSSAI licensed, and our chicken carries a No Antibiotics Ever mark.

Do you have vegetarian products?

Yes, eight of them, including potato kievs, cheese corn bites and veg burger patties. A paneer range is coming soon.

Who can buy from you?

Hotels, restaurants, QSR chains, caterers, modern trade and distributors. Use the form above to request samples.

How do I become a distributor?

Choose Distributor in the form and tell us your city and cold-storage capacity. The regional team will respond.
